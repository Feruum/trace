# TRACE visual system

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** TRACE is a verification workspace, not a generic university gallery; the first view shows search and evidence status together instead of hiding the mechanism behind marketing copy.

**OWN-WORLD:** Mineral off-white surfaces, ink-black navigation, electric teal evidence accent, amber uncertainty, hairline borders, compact rounded controls, and editorial image-led sections. No decorative gradients or invented hero imagery.

**STORY:** A prospective student sees what the open web can actually prove about a university, filters the evidence, saves profiles, and compares coverage without confusing coverage with quality.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact TRACE wordmark sits above a split workspace: a large search action occupies the left, while a proof/status rail on the right explains sources, timing, and the no-fabrication rule. The primary search action remains visible without scrolling on laptop and phone.

**FORM:** Operate-first evidence workspace, implemented as a public route with a search-to-profile transition and an optional persistent comparison tray.

## Durable rules

- Ink is the structural color. Teal marks source-backed actions and active evidence; amber marks review or missing data; red is reserved for operational failure.
- Use high-contrast type hierarchy: compact metadata, assertive profile names, readable body measure, and generous separation between evidence sections.
- Use borders and offset shadows intentionally; avoid nested card stacks and decorative blur.
- Every media treatment carries source, license, date state, and confidence in the same visual neighborhood.
- Empty categories explain the gap. They never render placeholder imagery.
- Motion belongs to state transitions: search begins the evidence run, loading exposes elapsed time, and compare opens as a deliberate tray. No idle wobble.
- All controls are keyboard reachable, use logical-direction utilities, and expose state beyond color.
- Responsive layout collapses evidence columns to one reading order at narrow widths; filters remain reachable and wrapping is acceptable.
